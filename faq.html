Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions people have when they start thinking about home cybersecurity.

Do I really need to care about this?

Yes. If you use the internet for banking, email, social media, or work, then basic security protects your money, identity, and privacy.

What are the first things I should do?

  1. Secure your Wi-Fi network with a strong password.
  2. Turn on updates and security features on your main devices.
  3. Improve the passwords on your most important accounts.

Is this only for “tech people”?

No. The whole point is to make security understandable for people who do not have a computer degree. The focus is on clear explanations and realistic steps.
